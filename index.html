Yes. Website updates are possible after launch. If you need to update content yourself from a dashboard, we can scope a CMS or admin panel as a separate feature.
Are domain and hosting included in the package?
Not automatically. Domain, hosting and third-party costs depend on the provider and plan, and will be listed separately in the quote.
How do I start a project?
