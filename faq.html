✦Frequently asked questions

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Explore the common details about a website project, from the first quote to ongoing updates.

How much does a website cost?
The proposed starting packages are ₹2,999 for a starter site, ₹4,999 for a business site and ₹9,999+ for e-commerce. A final quote depends on pages, content, functionality and integrations.
Is the domain and hosting included?
Not automatically. Domain registration and hosting depend on the provider and package selected. Your quote should clearly state what is included and who owns the accounts.
How long does a website take to build?
Timing depends on the agreed scope, content readiness, feedback, revision rounds and third-party services. We’ll agree on an estimated timeline before work begins.
Can I update my site after launch?
Yes. You can commission updates after launch. If you want to manage products, images and text yourself through a dashboard, a CMS or admin panel can be scoped separately.
Will the website work on mobile?
Responsive layouts are part of the planned build. The final site should be checked on common mobile, tablet and desktop viewport sizes.
Can you add payments, bookings or a dashboard?
These features can be discussed. They may require a database, backend development, third-party accounts, provider approval and additional costs.
Do you guarantee Google rankings?
No responsible service can guarantee exact rankings. SEO foundations can be implemented, but search results depend on competition, content, authority and many changing factors.
How does the enquiry form work?
The contact form validates the required fields and opens WhatsApp with your project details filled in. You can review and send the message yourself.
Are the portfolio samples live client sites?
No. The examples currently shown on this website are explicitly labelled demo concepts to illustrate possible design directions.
Can I request revisions?
Revision rounds and their scope should be written into the quotation so both sides know what is included.
